# Plans and pricing

Launch prices (hypotheses, not market-validated), defined once in `PlanSeeder`: Solo $29 (1 seat), Shop $59 (4 seats; the plan's key is still `start-up`, only its display name changed), Pro $119 (10 seats). Job board, quote history and AI quote drafting start at Shop; material tracking, qualification records and `Feature::AiAgent` (MCP access) are Pro, with the first two also sold as $25 add-ons. The default trial is 30 days (`PlatformSetting::DEFAULT_TRIAL_DAYS`); `Shop::TRIAL_DAYS` (14) remains only as the legacy baseline fallback. Extra seats: `plans.extra_seat_price_cents` ($9 on Shop and Pro, null on Solo = can't buy) and `shops.extra_seats`; `Shop::seatLimit()` = plan seats + extras (or the admin override), and `seatCapacity()` is what's enforced (trial shops get the top plan's seats). Enforced at invite (`SendShopInvitation`, pending invites hold a seat) and at accept; bought via `ShopSeatController` (owner-only, needs a live billing period, can't go below people already seated). Reducing seats is immediate, no refund. `PlanAdvisor` ignores seats.
